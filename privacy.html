Tiny Landlord — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Tiny Landlord is a single-player game. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Game data. Your progress is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us.

Ads. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, if you allow tracking), IP address (for approximate location), ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you decline tracking in the iOS prompt, you still see ads, but they are not personalized by cross-app tracking. In regions that require it, you will see a consent message from Google, and you can change your choice later in the game under Shop & settings → Ad privacy options. See Google's ad policy.

Purchases. The optional "Remove interstitials" purchase is processed by Apple. We receive no payment information.

Analytics. The game logs gameplay events on your device for debugging; this is not transmitted to us.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices. You can reset the advertising ID or turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Deleting the app deletes your saved game.
